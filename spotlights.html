Spotlight

Ms. Brandi Barnes

Veteran. Mom. Founder of Favor Forward. Owner of Designs by BCE.

Brandi served her country, and now she pours that same heart into her family and her business. She is also the founder of Favor Forward Foundation, turning compassion into practical action. Through Designs by BCE, she offers event services, custom gifts, and party favors, and all that comes with them, to make life’s big moments unforgettable, from baby showers and gender reveals to graduations and beyond.

Travel Partner

Chericka Johnson.

Meet Chericka Johnson.

Co-founder of Love + LeisureLuxury travel, weddings & retreats

Some of the best chapters are written somewhere new. My sister-in-law, Chericka Johnson, helps make those trips happen. She’s traveled to more than 35 countries, has planned travel for weddings and retreats with more than 100 guests, and brings over 20 years in corporate leadership, nonprofit strategy, and business ownership.

Chericka plans destination weddings, retreats, milestone celebrations, honeymoons, and vacations. Before she talks about where you’ll go, she wants to know why you’re going. That’s what makes the trip feel personal instead of packaged.

Planning a girls’ trip, a retreat, or the getaway you keep putting off? Let Chericka handle the details so you can just enjoy the trip.
