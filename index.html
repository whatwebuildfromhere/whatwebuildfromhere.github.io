Her Story
“I stopped waiting for permission to start over.”

There was a season when I kept rereading the same page of my life, hoping it would end differently. It didn’t change until I picked up the pen myself. That’s where Writing Her Next Chapter began: with one woman deciding her story wasn’t finished.

“I am fearfully and wonderfully made.”

Psalm 139:14

This is a home for women who are healing, rebuilding, and becoming. Bring your whole self, your questions, and your hope. We’ll turn the page together.

— Amanda

What this is

A women-only community built for healing and growth. It starts with intimate gatherings and grows into a conference where every woman has a seat at the table.
Building Women Up

We rise by lifting each other.

Writing Her Next Chapter is about more than one woman’s story. It’s about building real relationships. The kind where women show up for each other, open doors, and grow together. Bring a friend, bring your sister, bring the woman who needs it most. There’s room for all of us here.
