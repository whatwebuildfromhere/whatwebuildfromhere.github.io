Her Story
“I stopped waiting for permission to start over.”

There was a season when I kept rereading the same page of my life, hoping it would end differently. It didn’t change until I picked up the pen myself. That’s where Writing Her Next Chapter began: with one woman deciding her story wasn’t finished.

This is a home for women who are healing, rebuilding, and becoming. Bring your whole self, your questions, and your hope. We’ll turn the page together.

— Amanda
